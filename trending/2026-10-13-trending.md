## Editor's Take
Two repos stand out today. **`wheresryan22/anatomy`** is a Claude Code agent skill that hit 8K stars in under a week — it lets agents autonomously generate explanatory isometric/SVG/WebGL figures, self-checks output with headless Chrome, and works across Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, and Antigravity. This is a concrete signal that the "agent skills as reusable plugins" pattern is gaining real traction in the Claude ecosystem, and it's a natural content hook for showing what agent-native workflows look like. **`mhtsec/ARTEX`** is an autonomous multi-agent penetration testing system: a planner dispatches parallel worker agents that share an exploration graph and pass attack chains forward event-by-event. It's a compelling real-world example of agentic task decomposition at work — the kind of architecture your audience is actively trying to understand and build.
