## Editor's Take
Two things stand out this week. First, **zai-org/ZCode** landed 6,770 stars in just 5 days — it's a new coding agent harness from Z.ai that positions itself as a direct competitor to Claude Code and Cursor, with an extensibility pitch that's worth a dedicated breakdown. This "System 1 decision engine" pattern — fast, non-autoregressive, used for routing and classification inside agents — is becoming a recurring theme across multiple repos this week and is fertile ground for an explainer on the new inference stack that's forming around agent decision-making.
