## Editor's Take
Two repos this week are worth your full attention. `nexu-io/opengame` is an AI-native game studio that routes work across 20+ coding agents—Claude Code, Codex, DeepSeek—using an MCP-first architecture to build and ship games to WeChat, TikTok, and itch.io; it's a live demonstration of multi-agent orchestration that your audience would want to see dissected. `alesha-pro/llm.asm` is a Qwen3-0.6B inference engine and Mini-GPT trainer written entirely in x86-64 NASM assembly with zero C and zero libc—12 KB to run a transformer bare-metal—which makes for compelling "how small can an LLM actually get" content.
