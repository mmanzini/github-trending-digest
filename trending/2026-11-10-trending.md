# GitHub Trending – 2026-11-10 (Tuesday)

## Editor's Take

Two repos stand out this week. **DIYgod/pinky** ("Let AI agents use your phone, with you in the loop") is a Go project that bridges AI agents to mobile device control with explicit human-in-the-loop approval — a sharp illustration of the agentic trust and oversight problem that's dominating the conversation right now. **voyager-so/vork** is a headless 3D and VFX engine built specifically for agents, an early-stage Rust project suggesting the next frontier of agent capabilities is spatial/visual reasoning and content generation in 3D environments. In the 30-day window, **deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype** (21k+ stars) is DeepSeek's own take on autonomous agents — worth watching closely for what their architecture choices signal about the state of the art.
