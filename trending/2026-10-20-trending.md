# GitHub Trending – 2026-10-20 (Tuesday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos stand out this week for AI/agent content creators. **`Leonxlnx/earshot`** is the most novel: it gives coding agents an audio sense — let AI hear, review, and generate sound, MCP-native and CPU-only. This is a compelling "new modality for agents" story that's essentially unexplored in the content space. On the 30-day view, **`yetone/magpie`** (17.9k stars) continues to dominate: a macOS menu-bar tool that lets you run Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI on any backend model, which speaks directly to the cost and flexibility conversation your audience cares about. `deepseek-ai/DeepInfer` (12k stars in under 4 days) also warrants a watch — it's the official high-performance inference stack from the DeepSeek team and signals the direction of their model serving story.
