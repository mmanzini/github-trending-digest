Two repos this week are genuinely worth a closer look. First, **openai/NavierStokesAndEuler** is a landmark: OpenAI published Lean 4 formal certificates for Navier-Stokes and Euler fluid dynamics equations—one of the Millennium Prize Problems—marking a moment where AI-assisted formal reasoning has broken into frontier mathematics. For an AI content creator, this is a rare "before and after" story worth covering. It already has 1 K+ stars in under four days and could double as both a content creation tool and a demo of agentic multi-step pipelines.
