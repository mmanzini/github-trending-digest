# GitHub Trending – 2026-09-20 (Sunday)

## Editor's Take
The Jev story takes a new turn today: **NandhaKishorM/laya**, the non-autoregressive decision engine underlying the entire Jev ecosystem, jumped from ~1.2k to nearly 4k stars overnight as developers started studying the architecture rather than just using the tools on top of it. Laya's core insight — one forward pass through a transformer encoder yields calibrated, multilingual decisions in 33ms with zero hallucination risk — is the foundation worth understanding deeply, and **mizorewww/laya-mlx** (new today, already at 1.6k stars) confirms Apple Silicon developers are already porting it. For Claude Code–focused content, **tamaratran/fast-jev-compaction** (now at 5k stars) remains the most immediately actionable pick: it's a drop-in plugin that replaces default context compaction with Jev-scored decisions, keeping relevant tool calls verbatim and dropping stale ones — a concrete, demonstrable improvement to Claude Code's context management that will resonate with any practitioner audience.
