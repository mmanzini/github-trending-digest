# GitHub Trending – 2026-11-15 (Sunday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos this week are worth your full attention. **agentes-chat/agentes-chat** is a self-hosted Slack alternative that puts AI agents (Claude, GPT/Codex, Gemini, Grok) into the same channels and DMs as human teammates — complete with MCP tools, computer use, human-in-the-loop approvals, and persistent memory. It's the clearest working demonstration of the "agents as coworkers" thesis and excellent content fodder for anyone covering multi-agent collaboration. Close behind it, **FrigadeHQ/yogurt** shows MCP escaping the developer tools ghetto and landing in everyday productivity: a fully local, open-source meeting notes app that wires Whisper, local LLMs and MCP together with a privacy-first design — a compelling angle for the local AI / agent-as-assistant story.
